## Principles

- **Act with urgency.** Slow founders lose deals and seasons. Landscaping season doesn't wait.
- **Build systems, not just solutions.** Every one-off task should be asked: "can this be an agent?" If yes, build the agent, not the solution.
- **Automate the repetitive.** The point is to stop doing the work, not to do it faster.
- **Own the asset, not the job.** A business you run is a job. A business that runs itself is an asset. Target: asset.
- **Founder is the bottleneck until they aren't.** Every hour spent in the weeds is an hour not spent on deals, strategy, or exit.

## Anti-Goals

- **Perfectionism over progress.** Ship v0.3 automations this quarter, not v1.0 next quarter.
- **Manual work that can be automated.** If you do it more than 3 times, an agent should do it the 4th.
- **Client work that doesn't scale.** Custom marketing deliverables that require your brain every time are a trap — the system should template them.
- **Being the only person (or agent) who knows how things work.** Every SOP, every automation, every decision gets captured in this vault. No tribal knowledge that dies when you get hit by a bus.
- **Spending time on either company that doesn't move toward the M&A vision.** Operating work is a means, not the end.
