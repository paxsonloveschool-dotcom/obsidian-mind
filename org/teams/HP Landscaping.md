## Season Rhythm

Landscaping work is shaped by the calendar. The vault should be aware of it:

| Quarter | Mode | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 (Jan-Mar) | Planning + prep | Estimates for season, equipment maintenance, client renewals, marketing |
| Q2 (Apr-Jun) | **Peak execution** | Delivery, crew dispatch, dawn-to-dusk jobs, invoicing cycle |
| Q3 (Jul-Sep) | Peak execution | Delivery, hardscape installs, mid-season reviews |
| Q4 (Oct-Dec) | Wind-down + prep | Leaf cleanup, equipment winterize, plan for next year, M&A reading |

The Q2 automation buildout is happening during peak execution — every hour saved is an hour the owner gets back during the busiest season.
