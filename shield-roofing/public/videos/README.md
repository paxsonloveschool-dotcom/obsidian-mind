# Video Assets for Top Shield Roofing

## Hero Background Video

Place your professional roofing video footage in this directory.

### Required File:
- **Filename:** `roofing-drone-footage.mp4`
- **Location:** `/public/videos/roofing-drone-footage.mp4`

### Video Specifications:
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec recommended)
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) or higher
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Duration:** 30-60 seconds (will loop automatically)
- **File Size:** Recommended under 10MB for optimal loading
- **Frame Rate:** 24-30 fps

### Content Recommendations:
The video should showcase:
- Aerial/drone footage of completed roofing projects
- Close-up shots of quality shingle work
- Professional roofers at work
- Before/after transitions
- Texas residential properties
- Smooth, cinematic camera movements

### Video Optimization Tips:
1. Compress the video to reduce file size while maintaining quality
2. Remove audio track (video plays muted)
3. Use tools like HandBrake or Adobe Media Encoder for optimization
4. Consider using a video CDN for faster loading

### Alternative:
If you don't have video ready yet, the site will automatically fall back to displaying a static image background until the video file is added.

### How to Add Your Video:
1. Export your final video as MP4
2. Name it `roofing-drone-footage.mp4`
3. Place it in this `/public/videos/` directory
4. The website will automatically use it on the homepage hero section

### Example Video Sources:
- Hire a local drone operator in College Station or Houston
- Use stock footage websites (Pexels, Pixabay, Videvo)
- Film your own B-roll with a smartphone or camera
- Commission a professional videographer
