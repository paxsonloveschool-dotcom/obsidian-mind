# Google Rethink Sessions

Two Google keynotes, Rethink Retail (Sep 16, 2026) and Rethink ROI (Sep 17, 2026). Goal: a tips and tricks list Restore can use on client Google Ads. Retail ideas fit ecommerce clients. ROI ideas fit lead gen and service clients.

## Tips and Tricks

### Bidding and budgets
- **Cap on margin, not on spend.** Agree a margin or ROAS floor with finance instead of a fixed daily cap. If a campaign shuts off at 7pm while profitable buyers are still shopping at 8pm, the sale goes to a competitor. (Retail [24:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1440))
- **Demand-led budget pacing.** Keep a monthly budget but let Google spend more on high-demand days and less on slow ones. Black Friday and Cyber Monday demand can run 3 to 7x a normal day. (Retail [2:08:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7680))
- **Missed opportunities report.** Shows what the account would have earned if budget or bid hadn't been capped. Pull it before any budget talk with a client. (Retail [2:09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7740))
- **Smart Bidding Exploration.** Lets the ROAS target flex 10 to 30% so the bidder can test new queries, still within your targeting. Google says up to 27% more unique converters at a 10% flex. It's coming to Shopping and PMax with a retail feed. (Retail [2:05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7500))
- **Product value optimization** (closed beta). Raises the value of chosen products by category, brand or custom label, such as high-margin items, clearance or "zombie SKUs" that get no traffic, without splitting campaigns. Reporting shows adjusted and original value. (Retail [23:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1380), [1:59:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7170))
- **New customer acquisition goal.** Now on every campaign type, with a "new customer only" mode and a new "prospects" mode for people who have never engaged. Three setup steps: upload fresh Customer Match lists, install the new customer tag, and set realistic values. (Retail [1:55:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=6900))
- **Loyalty goal.** Set member pricing or free shipping in Merchant Center, upload matching tier lists through Data Manager, then turn on the goal, optionally with a value multiplier. The benefits show on the shopping ads. (Retail [1:57:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7050))
- **Journey-aware bidding** (beta, Search with tCPA). If you track the form fill but bid to a deeper, sparse conversion, Google now uses the form fill as an early signal. You don't need to set anything up. (ROI [1:48:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6480))
- **Bid to a deep, valuable conversion.** Don't bid to a shallow web form. Cost per lead looks like a vanity metric to a CFO. (ROI [48:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2880), [1:43:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6210))
- **Cheap value-based bidding test.** Even without a full CRM upload, give each conversion type its own value. For example, Snyk valued a demo request higher than a content download and got about 20% lift. (ROI [46:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2760))
- **Long sales cycles.** When the sale lands weeks after the lead, the delay hurts bidding. Model a predicted value early and feed it back through value-based bidding. Level Agency does this with "Level Signal", and The Zebra pushed carriers to send outcome data sooner. (ROI [43:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2610), [1:04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=3870))

### AI Max, Performance Max and Shopping
- **Turn on AI Max for Search.** Google says it averages about 15% more conversion value, or 15% more conversions at the same budget with all features on. One advertiser with a huge keyword list still found 30% more volume. (Retail [21:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1260), ROI [7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=420))
- **AI Max for Shopping** (open beta). It's a single toggle with two parts. Text customization rewrites ad titles from the feed. Final URL expansion can send broad queries like "best houseplant for beginners" to a category page. Google claims about 5% more conversion value. Test it before peak season. (Retail [1:50:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=6630))
- **Brand exclusions in AI Max for Shopping** keep branded searches in the campaigns where you want them. Exact-match Search still takes priority over Shopping, same as with PMax. (Retail [1:54:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=6840))
- **Guardrails without choking the campaign.** Use text guidelines, text exclusions and messaging restrictions now. They carry over into AI Brief, a plain-language brief about who to reach and what never to say, once it reaches the account. Don't over-restrict, because that blocks good longer queries. (Retail [22:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1350), [2:03:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=7380))
- **Regulated clients can still use AI copy.** Infinity Sales Group, a telecom reseller, used text guidelines for partner and geographic rules and got about 40% more revenue. (ROI [9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=540))
- **AI Max takes commitment.** Chime: "it does not work out of the box." Give it one dedicated owner rather than splitting it across many people, iterate, and stick with it. (ROI [1:09:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4170))

### Product feed and Merchant Center
- **Feed quality comes first.** Merchants who follow the feed best practices see about 5% more conversions the next month. Almost every new AI feature runs off the feed. (Retail [17:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1020))
- **Conversational attributes.** There are 6 new Merchant Center fields, such as product Q&A and related products, that feed AI Mode answers. Shortcut: Lululemon uploaded the product PDFs it already had. (Retail [18:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1080))
- **AI performance insights** in Merchant Center shows how products do on AI surfaces and what to fix in descriptions. (Retail [19:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1170))
- **Add the product feed to Demand Gen.** Google says up to 35% more conversions, and it's the same feed you already use for Shopping and PMax. (Retail [1:49:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=6570))

### YouTube and Demand Gen
- **Run Search and YouTube together.** People who see something on social check it on Google first, and 2 in 3 end up buying a different brand. Creating demand without Search to catch it hands sales to competitors. Rippling learned this the hard way. (Retail [15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=900), ROI [1:13:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4410))
- **Layer Demand Gen on top of PMax and Shopping** for discovery queries like "best gifts for runners". Google says Demand Gen now averages 30% more conversions than last year. (Retail [22:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1320), [1:48:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=6510))
- **Demand Gen in four steps:** give it good data, tell it the goal, vary the creative, and let it find new customers. (Retail [1:08:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=4110))
- **Judge YouTube by its effect on Search, not by last click.** Use attributed branded searches, search lift, or brand lift. Columbia Southern saw 111% more brand searches and 5x conversion rates after adding Demand Gen. Rippling saw 160% more branded search and 26% more qualified leads. (ROI [11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=690), [1:14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4440))
- **Messy creative works.** StockX's best ROAS ads in Q4 were cheap 15, 30 and 60 second cutdowns of podcast footage its creative team didn't like. Wayfair found polished and low-fi videos both perform. Don't commit to one format. (Retail [1:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3600), [1:02:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3720))
- **Show the product doing its job.** Wayfair's example is an outdoor sofa with cushion storage. Gap's lesson: every viral piece needs the product front and center. (Retail [52:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3150), [1:27:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=5250))
- **Creator tools:** YouTube Open Call for briefing native creators, and Affiliate Boost, which keeps a creator's affiliate link live when you put ad spend behind their video. Wayfair was first to test both. (Retail [59:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3570), [1:04:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3870))
- **Low-cost YouTube creative:** Veo and Nano Banana are built into Google Ads Asset Studio. One B2B lead aggregator reports 90% lower CPA after using it to launch Demand Gen. Canva repurposed its social videos. (ROI [12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=750))
- **New ad formats coming:** Business agent for YouTube ads lets viewers ask questions inside the ad. Business agent for leads adds a chat to Search ads that can pre-qualify a lead and pre-fill its details. (Retail [42:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=2550), ROI [28:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=1710))

### Measurement and data (strongest section for lead gen clients)
- **Upgrade to Google Tag Gateway.** It serves the tag from the client's own domain through their CDN or CMS, so browsers block less and more conversions get recorded. The speakers quoted about 14% more conversions and called it a no-brainer. (ROI [1:30:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=5430), [1:41:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6060))
- **Use Data Manager as the single place to bring data in.** It's under Google Ads > Tools. It has ready-made connectors (Salesforce and Shopify were named) plus Google Cloud, AWS and Snowflake. One upload covers both measurement and Customer Match, and it shows row-level errors. Move old OCI uploads over to it. (ROI [1:32:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=5550), [1:55:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6930))
- **Enhanced conversions.** The web and leads versions are being merged into one. Send through both the tag and the API. The UI now shows each account's own uplift from it. (ROI [1:36:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=5790))
- **Import the whole funnel:** MQL, SQL and closed won, not just the web form. Then add more fields to those uploads over time to improve match rates. (ROI [1:44:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6270))
- **Check tag health monthly** with Tag Assistant. The new visual tagging lets a marketer set up events by clicking through the site, with no developer queue. Keep a change log so one person isn't overwriting another. (ROI [1:27:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=5220), [1:54:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6840))
- **Lead intent scoring** (pilot) tags each lead high, medium or low using CRM history and Google signals, with about 90% accuracy. It's for sales prioritization only and isn't used in bidding. **Lead journey mapping** is now generally available. (ROI [29:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=1770), [1:46:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6360))
- **Measure in the right order.** Start with incrementality tests, then calibrate MMM and attribution against them. Google's open-source MMM, Meridian, now includes experiment calibration. (ROI [34:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2070), [1:53:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6810))
- **Agree on one brand metric with finance and stick with it.** Branded search lift is the easiest. (ROI [35:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2130))
- **"How did you hear about us?" at checkout.** Keep it short, two clicks with randomized options. StockX found creator content driving Google searches this way, which last-click reporting would have missed. (Retail [53:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3210))
- **Start small and prove it.** Win with one easy upgrade, such as Tag Gateway or valued conversions, show the result, then ask the client for CRM access. Level Agency won't take a client without CRM data. (ROI [39:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=2340), [1:42:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=6120))

### SEO and AI search (organic)
- **Good SEO is good GEO.** AI Mode and AI Overviews run on the normal ranking systems. Skip tactics aimed at bots, generic filler pages and fake forum mentions. Publish first-hand experience and real expertise, and keep the website's facts easy to find. (Retail [33:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=2010), ROI [24:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=1470))
- **AI search queries look different.** AI Mode queries run about 3x longer than classic searches, and more than 1 in 6 use voice or an image. Write ad copy and pages that answer whole questions. (Retail [32:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=1920))
- **Local and in-store.** "Near me" and "in stock" are the most common follow-up questions about stores in AI Mode. Keep Google Maps and Business Profile data current. (Retail [16:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=960))
- Chime says its traffic from AI search converts at about 2x its regular organic traffic. (ROI [1:08:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4110))

### Selling it to the client or CFO
- Use incrementality, not last click, as the scoreboard when YouTube or upper-funnel spend is on the table. Bring in finance early so they share the same KPI. (Retail [53:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OJPyIUL_vbo&t=3210), ROI [1:12:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4350))
- Rename brand spend in performance terms. Chime called mid-funnel YouTube "non-last-click performance media" and Rippling called it "enhanced performance marketing". Both backed it with incrementality tests. (ROI [1:15:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4500), [1:18:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZ5oX9nKztk&t=4710))

### Quick checklist for Restore accounts
1. Tag health check, then Google Tag Gateway, then enhanced conversions.
2. Connect the CRM in Data Manager and bid on the deepest conversion with values.
3. Turn on AI Max (Search, plus Shopping for ecommerce) with text guidelines as guardrails.
4. Enrich the Merchant Center feed and add it to Demand Gen.
5. Move from fixed daily caps to demand-led pacing with a margin floor, and use the missed opportunities report to justify it.
6. Track branded search lift as the proof that YouTube is working.

All stats above are Google's own figures or case studies shown on stage, so treat them as vendor claims.
